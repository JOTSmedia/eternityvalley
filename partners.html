Partner with the Rainbow Bridge

You meet families on the hardest day of their pet's life. Give them a gentle next step.

Somewhere Over the Rainbow Bridge is a virtual memorial service: families place a photorealistic memorial anywhere on Earth — their backyard, a favorite beach — or in our Rainbow Bridge Valley sanctuary. Friends visit, light candles, and leave gifts — and a share of every plot, membership and gift goes to an animal charity the family chooses, with every cent published in a public ledger.

Veterinary clinics & hospices

Include a memorial card with your aftercare materials. Families create a free-to-visit memorial; you're remembered as the practice that cared beyond the end.

Cremation & aftercare services

Offer a digital memorial alongside urns and paw prints — a place the whole family, everywhere, can visit together.

Shelters & rescues

Become a listed charity: memorial owners choose you, and a share of every gift flows to your animals. Zero cost to join.

Pet businesses

Groomers, boarders, pet shops — honor customers' companions and stay part of their story.

How referrals work

You get a personal link — oursite.com/?ref=your-clinic — for cards, emails, and aftercare packets. Every visit and purchase from your link is tracked, and partners earn a revenue share on first purchases (terms on request). No integration work: a link or QR code is everything.

Charity partners

A share of everything goes to the charity the family chooses — not gifts alone. Plots, memorial items and gifts each carry 10%; memberships 5%; merchandise 15%. Straight donations and the dedicated "Shelter Donation" gift are passed through at 100%. Every share is calculated on the amount left after card processing, so "100%" is the amount that actually lands in your account, not a headline figure we quietly deduct from.

Families who never buy anything can still raise money for you: anyone can start a free fundraising campaign in their animal's name, choose a charity, and send every cent of it on. No plot, no membership, no account.

Charities are onboarded with verification and receive monthly payouts with full reporting. Every transaction — what came in, what the processor took, what reached a charity — is published in a running public ledger, hash-chained so that entries cannot be quietly rewritten after the fact. Your payouts are auditable by anyone, including you.
