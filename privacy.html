What we collect

Account data (name, email) via Firebase Authentication; memorial content you create (pet details, epitaphs, photos, locations you choose); guestbook messages and gifts; payment records via Stripe (we never see full card numbers); basic usage events (purchases, page activity) for our admin dashboard.

Location

Memorial coordinates you choose are public — that is the point of the map. Your device location is requested only for the optional live-weather ambience, is used in the browser to fetch a weather code from Open-Meteo, and is never stored on our servers.

Third parties

Google Maps Platform (map rendering, geocoding — subject to Google's privacy policy), Esri (fallback satellite imagery), Firebase (auth, database), Stripe (payments), Open-Meteo (weather, anonymous). We do not sell personal data and do not show third-party advertising.

Public vs private

Public: memorial content, its location, guestbook entries, gift counts, and the display name you give. Private: your email, purchase history, and authentication data.

Your rights

You can delete a memorial or your account at any time (contact support); deletion removes public content within 30 days, subject to payment-record retention required by law. EU/UK and California residents have additional access/erasure rights — email us to exercise them.

Children

The Service is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.
