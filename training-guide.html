Staff Training Guide

A compassionate guide to introducing Somewhere Over the Rainbow Bridge to grieving pet parents.

The Sacred Moment

When a family says goodbye to a beloved companion, they are experiencing profound loss. Our role is to provide a gentle, eternal sanctuary where their memory can live on. This guide helps your staff introduce Eternity Valley with grace and empathy.

How to Introduce the Platform

Example Script:
"We know how hard today is. Many of our families find comfort in having a quiet place to remember them. We've partnered with a beautiful digital sanctuary called Somewhere Over the Rainbow Bridge. You can create a memorial for [Pet's Name] in their favorite spot—like your backyard or the park they loved—and every tribute helps a living animal in need."

Key Points for Staff

  • Compassion First: Always read the room. If a family is overwhelmed, simply place the Sympathy Card Insert with their discharge papers or memorial items, without a prolonged explanation.
  • Free and Accessible: Emphasize that creating a memorial fundraising campaign or signing the guestbook is completely free.
  • Giving Back: Mention that 100% of campaign donations pass directly through to verified animal charities (like the ASPCA or Best Friends Animal Society).
  • Global Presence: Families can choose a plot in the virtual 3D Sanctuary, or pin a marker to any real-world location using the globe view.

Using the Partner Materials

  • Sympathy Card Inserts: Include these inside the sympathy cards your clinic mails out, or hand them directly to clients along with paw prints or ashes. Make sure they feature your clinic's unique QR code.
  • Waiting Room Poster: Place the poster in your comfort room or waiting area, allowing clients to discover the sanctuary quietly on their own time.

Support and Assistance

If a client has questions about setting up their memorial, gently direct them to visit the URL on their card. The Eternity Valley website provides a simple, guided walkthrough to help them consecrate their memorial.
