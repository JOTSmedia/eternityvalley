Privacy Policy

How your information is handled on this site

Last updated 20 August 2026

What this covers

This policy explains what happens to the information you send through this website. It is written to be read, not to be skimmed past.

What is collected

Only what you type into a form and choose to send:

  • Contact and booking forms — your name, email address, phone number if you give one, your preferred session type and date, and whatever you write in the message field.
  • Newsletter signup — your email address.
  • Sacred bag — the items you add are stored in your own browser and are never sent anywhere until you place an order.

There are no analytics scripts, no advertising pixels and no third-party trackers on this site. Fonts and images are served from this domain rather than a CDN, so loading a page does not tell anyone else that you visited.

Why it is collected

To reply to you, to arrange and deliver a session you asked for, and to send the newsletter if you asked for it. That is all. Your information is never sold, rented or shared for anyone else's marketing.

What you write in a message

People often share something personal when enquiring about a reading — a bereavement, a health worry, a relationship. That is treated as confidential. It is read by Beth Elise, used to prepare for your session, and not discussed with anyone else.

How long it is kept

Enquiries and booking records are kept while there is an active or likely working relationship, and are deleted on request. Newsletter subscriptions are kept until you unsubscribe.

Your choices

You can ask what is held about you, ask for it to be corrected, or ask for it to be deleted. Write to hello@bethelisepsychic.com and it will be handled.

Children

Sessions are offered to adults. This site is not directed at children and information is not knowingly collected from anyone under 16.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
